Order type

Market orders are only sent 9:30 am – 4:00 pm. Outside those hours the algo always sends a limit order instead (brokers take only limit orders then).

Limit price

Buys are priced this much above the last trade, sells this much below (at least one cent), so they fill quickly. A negative number waits for a better price instead — for entries only: exits (exit rule, stop-loss, trailing stop) are always priced to fill. Spreads are wider outside regular hours: a small number there often goes unfilled.

Cancel if not filled after

An unfilled limit order is cancelled — not an error. If the rule still says buy or sell, the algo tries again later.

Trailing stop

The algo watches every trade and sends the exit order (limit or market, as above) when the price falls this far from its best since the entry (rises, for a short). It is not held at the broker, so it only works while the algo is running.

Extra cost outside regular hours

Paper trading and backtests only: fills in pre-market, after-hours and overnight cost this much more, for the wider spreads.
