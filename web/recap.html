A 2-3 minute video of everything since the last 4 pm close: after-hours (4-8 pm, SMART),
    overnight (8 pm-3:50 am, Blue Ocean, IBKR's overnight venue) and pre-market (4-9:30 am, SMART), then where
    things stand. Made by the Mac from the board's own price record and sent to your phone every trading day
    at 8:05 am New York time (change JB_RECAP_TIME in settings.env). The last 14 are kept.
    It also gives the market weather: each sector fund gets a word from its move since the close
    (sunny up 1% or more, partly sunny, cloudy = about flat, rainy, stormy down 1% or more).

  
WEEKThe weekly recap comes every Friday at 8:15 pm New York time
    (JB_WEEKLY_TIME in settings.env; if the Mac was asleep it catches up until Sunday 6 pm): the week's best and worst,
    the market weather for the week, how the Picks and your own trades did, the best paper algo strategy, and next
    week's earnings for your stocks and the big economic events. The last 8 are kept.
