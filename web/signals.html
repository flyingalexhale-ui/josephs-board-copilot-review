Recent signals

SEC filings (8-K, insider buys, 13D)

8-K = the company announcing something big (results, a deal, a CEO change, layoffs...). Insider buy = an executive or director buying shares with their own money (sales and stock awards are left out; buys are the rarer, stronger signal). 13D = an investor now owns 5%+ and may push for changes. From SEC EDGAR, checked every 15 minutes. Only filings after this was installed are listed.

Social buzz (StockTwits)

Posts per hour about each stock on StockTwits, compared with its normal pace over the last week (needs about 6 hours of checks to learn "normal"). A sudden jump often comes before or with a big move. Each stock is checked about every 20-30 minutes. Reddit is left out: it blocks free automatic reading.

Earnings today / last night

Typical move = the average size of the stock's move around its last 4 reports (close the day before to close the day after). Now = vs the 4 pm close. When the move passes the typical one you get a push. Report dates from Nasdaq.
