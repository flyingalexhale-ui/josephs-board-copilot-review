Uses the board's own minute record of the symbol (up to 35 days, every session; one price per minute). It runs about 35 backtests in the background, so it can take 10 to 20 minutes on the Mac; you can leave the page and come back.
        Grid: the two numbers from 60% to 140% of the file's value; green = made money. Mostly green = robust; one green square = luck. A row or column that is all the same means that number never came into play (another rule closed the trade first).
        Walk-forward: the best of 9 settings is chosen on the older 70% and tested on the newer 30% it never saw.
        Monte Carlo: the trades reshuffled 1,000 times: realistic worst drop and longest losing streak.
