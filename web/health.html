Parts of the board

Restart does the same as ./jb restart <name> in Terminal on the Mac: it stops that part and starts it again with the current settings.env. A price bridge takes about 30 seconds to get prices flowing again (IBKR needs IB Gateway running and logged in). Restarting the board itself makes this page go blank for a few seconds; it comes back by itself. Things that are "off in settings.env" are turned off on purpose.

Last price = the newest price the board got from that broker. During market hours it should be under a couple of minutes old. IBKR also has prices overnight (Sunday 8 pm to Friday 8 pm); Webull has none from 8 pm to 4 am on your plan, so an old Webull time overnight is normal.
