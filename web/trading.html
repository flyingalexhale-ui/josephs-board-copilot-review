Today

A trading day starts at 8 pm ET the evening before, so it runs overnight → pre-market → regular → after-hours, the order you trade it.

Trading journal

Your fills come in by themselves: IBKR every 5 minutes (from IB Gateway, the day's fills) and Webull every 10 minutes (last 7 days, if your Webull keys allow reading orders). You can also add a fill by hand.

Fills are paired into finished trades (first in, first out). Give each trade a tag (what kind of setup it was) and a note; the tables below then show which setups and which sessions actually make you money.

Position-size calculator

Decide how much you're willing to lose if the stop is hit; this works out how many shares that allows. Risk per share = entry − stop. Shares = money at risk ÷ risk per share.

Daily loss limit

Your limit: when today's closed trades plus your open positions are down more than this, your phone gets a push (once a day).

Also pause the algos: at the same moment, both paper algos are stopped. Stopping closes the algo's position and it opens nothing new until you press Resume on its algo tab.

Algo limit: each paper algo is stopped on its own (with a push) when it is down more than this today.

Paper algo leaderboard

Every trading day at 8:30 pm ET, each strategy file of both algos is run on paper over the board's own minute-by-minute prices (the last 35 days, overnight included). Each day's result is kept, so this ranks the strategies over weeks.

Ranked by the last 20 days. Green days = days it made money. running = the one that algo is trading on paper right now. To try a variation, copy a strategy file in the algo's strategies folder and change its numbers; it joins the race the next evening.

The board keeps one price per minute, so fills are a little rougher than real ones. Paper only, never real orders.

"What if" replay

Replay a past night minute by minute, from 4 pm ET to 10:30 am the next trading day, from the board's own record (35 days). Press Buy, Short or Flat as it plays to practice; your practice result shows at the end. Nothing is sent to a broker.

Your own fills and the board's prices only. For practice and record keeping, not advice.
