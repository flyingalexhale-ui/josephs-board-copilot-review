Market clock

The overnight mood mixes moves since the last 4 pm New York close: S&P and Nasdaq futures, Asia, Europe and Bitcoin count up when they rise; the fear index (VIX), the dollar and gold count the other way, because they usually rise when people get nervous. Above +25 = risk-on (buyers in charge), below −25 = risk-off (sellers in charge), in between = mixed. It's a quick read, not a prediction.

Since 4 pm = change since the last US 4 pm close (on weekends, since Friday 4 pm). Day = change vs that market's own previous day. The little line = the last 24 hours. Prices are free and delayed (about 10-20 min for futures and indexes) from Yahoo Finance, refreshed every 5 minutes, so use your IBKR tab for live ES / NQ / YM.

Economic calendar · next 7 days (New York time)

HIGH often moves the whole market (jobs report, inflation (CPI), Fed decisions). MED can move it. Forecast = what economists expect; Prev = last time. A number far from the forecast is what moves prices. Actual numbers show here only when the free feed includes them; otherwise check the News tab right after the release. Treasury auctions (the government borrowing money) can move interest rates and futures, especially 10-, 20- and 30-year ones. Sources: Forex Factory (free), TreasuryDirect, the Federal Reserve.
