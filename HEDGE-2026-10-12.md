# Delta hedging (branch hedge-2026-10-12)

One commit per part (see `git log main..hedge-2026-10-12`). Not merged to main, not installed on the Mac yet.
Everything uses the board's own prices (the IBKR / Webull boards' minute record and saved daily closes) and free
sources (Yahoo's daily closes only where the board has too little history). No paid data, no paid options.

## Part 1: Net Exposure line

Your total market delta across both brokers, as dollars gained or lost per 1% move in the Nasdaq-100 and in the
S&P 500. Read only.

* Each holding: value x leverage x beta. Leverage from the Night desk twins list (`gateway/mdata.py`): 3x funds 3,
  inverse funds negative (SQQQ -3). Beta of what the fund follows to QQQ / SPY, measured on the board's own record:
  half-hour moves in regular hours (at least 100), else daily closes, else Yahoo's daily closes, else a fixed
  estimate (shown as "Estimated Beta"). IBKR futures (MNQ, MES, NQ, ES, M2K, MYM...) count at full size.
* Holdings: `portfolio.positions()` (IBKR + Webull from `gateway/brokerpos.py`, plus typed-in trades for symbols no
  broker holds) and the IBKR futures, which `brokerpos.py` now keeps apart in `data/broker_positions.json`
  (`ibkr.futs`, not shown as Portfolio rows).
* Shown on the Portfolio tab (above Positions), the My Trading tab (under its menu) and the Phone page (a card under
  Your Holdings). **Details** lists every holding with its numbers.

Files: `gateway/hedge.py` (new Plus module `hedge`, `GET /api/plus/hedge`), `web/hedge-ui.js` (new), one
`<script>` line each in `web/portfolio.html`, `web/trading.html`, `web/phone.html`; `gateway/plus.py` (module
list), `gateway/brokerpos.py` (futures kept apart), `web/legend.js` (Delta Hedging section).

Tests: `python3 tests/hedge-test.py` (made-up prices with known answers: TQQQ = 3x QQQ, SOXX = 1.5x QQQ...).

## Part 2: Hedge calculator

For your current holdings: how many shares of an inverse fund, or how many MNQ / MES micro futures (IBKR only), bring
your Nasdaq-100 (or S&P 500) exposure to about flat. Never places an order.

* Choices: the index's own inverse funds (SQQQ, QID, PSQ / SPXU, SPXS, SDS, SH), the sector inverse of each group
  you hold (SOXS for SOXL, TECS for TECL...), and MNQ / MES. Net short? Bull funds and buying futures instead. The
  board never opens a short, so a fund is only ever bought.
* Each choice: shares / contracts (rounded; what is left per 1% after rounding), cost (buying power; futures: what one
  contract controls and a rough 5-10% margin range, marked as an estimate), daily decay of leveraged funds
  ((L^2 - L)/2 x daily variance of what it follows + a 0.95% yearly fee), hours, and the swing cut: how much smaller
  your half-hour moves would have been with this hedge on the board's own record. Best Match = biggest swing cut,
  ties go to the least buying power.
* Copy To Ticket fills the board's order ticket (new `fill()` hook in `web/ticket-ui.js`); nothing is sent until
  Check order, then Send. Futures: Copy Order Text (the ticket takes stocks and ETFs only).
* `GET /api/plus/hedge/calc?idx=ndx|spx&pct=25..100`. Portfolio tab (under Positions) and the phone card.

## Part 3: Hedge partners for what you hold

For each stock or fund you hold: the best hedge partner and the others, with how well each hedged on the board's own
record.

* Partners: the inverse twin (SQQQ for TQQQ; TQQQ, the "bull twin", for SQQQ), the sector inverse of what the fund
  follows (or, for a plain stock, the sector inverses the board has prices for, measured), the index inverse
  (SQQQ / SPXU) and the micro futures (MNQ / MES, IBKR only). A long inverse fund gets bull funds as partners.
* Record: correlation of daily returns (half-hours when there are too few days), the hedge ratio that cut the daily
  swings most ($ of partner per $ of holding), the daily swing cut with it, the overnight swing cut (4 pm close to the
  next 9:30 open, from the minute record, same ratio), the shares or whole contracts for your position (futures
  cuts are recomputed for the rounded contract count; "too big" when one contract is more than the position needs),
  and the weekly decay of 3x funds (the hedge's, and the holding's own).
* Best partner = the biggest average of the daily and overnight cuts; within 3 points, the cheaper one to hold.
* `GET /api/plus/hedge/pairs`. Portfolio tab, section Hedge Partners (Copy To Ticket as in the calculator).

## Part 4: Hedge ideas to trade (research, not advice)

A ranked list of pair ideas on the My Trading tab, backtested on the board's own minute record (35 days) and daily
closes. Clearly labelled research, not advice. Nothing here places an order.

* Ideas: each fund you hold, the algos' symbols, and every leveraged fund the board records, with each partner from
  Part 3 (inverse twin, sector inverse, index inverse, micro future) plus the inverse of the bull fund it moves with
  most (half-hour correlation 0.8 or more). Rules: hold the pair through the session, or buy on a 1% / 2% dip vs the
  4 pm close. Hedge size from the board's own history (the ratio that cut daily swings most).
* Sessions: after-hours, overnight, pre-market (entered in the first 30 minutes, closed 15 minutes before the session
  ends), plus a day hold on daily closes. Costs: 0.1% per fund fill outside regular hours (the algos' default),
  0.02% per futures fill. Per session: trades, win rate, average, total, worst drop, best, worst; ranked by the
  steadiest session (average / spread x square root of trades, at least 8 trades), at most 4 ideas per fund.
* Worked out in the background every 6 hours (Run Now on the tab), saved in `data/hedge_ideas.json`.
* Send To Run Backtest: writes `strategies/hedge_<fund>_<partner>_<rule>_<session>.yaml` in the chosen algo
  (entry / exit rules from the algo's clock and vs_close indicators, plus a `hedge:` section) and starts that algo's
  backtest on the board's record for that session (`gateway/algoctl.py` now takes `recorded` and `sessions`).
  Futures and overnight ideas go to IBKR only.
* Backtests of any strategy with a `hedge:` section now add the hedge leg (`algo/autohedge.py`, both algos;
  `strategy.py`, `backtest.py`, `bt_cli.py`, `run_backtest.py`): opened with each entry, closed with each exit, from
  the board's record of the hedge (MNQ / MES use NQ=F / ES=F), at the same costs. The algo tab shows the result alone
  and With Hedge (`web/hedge-algo.js`, one script line in `web/algo.html`).
* Tests (`tests/hedge-test.py`): ranking, per-session metrics, a twin pair costing exactly its fees, the dip rule,
  futures only at IBKR, and a real end-to-end run: Send To Run Backtest on a copy of ibkr-algo, the backtest on the
  board's record with the hedge leg, and the refusals.

## Part 5: Auto-Hedge button per algo

On each algo tab (Webull and IBKR), in the Paper / live trading card: one click turns on automatic hedging for that
algo's position, with the instrument and hedge ratio in a small box. **Default OFF.**

* Settings: `<algo folder>/hedge_settings.json`, written by the board (`POST /api/plus/hedge/algo`). Auto picks the
  fund that cut the symbol's daily swings most on the board's record (the inverse twin when there is no record);
  any fitting fund, or MNQ / MES at IBKR only. The hedge size ($ of the hedge per $1 of the position) comes from the
  board's own prices, times the ratio (10-150%).
* The algo (`algo/autohedge.py`, called from `core_trader.py` every 2 seconds): opens the hedge right after the
  position's buy fills, makes it smaller on a part exit, closes it with the position. Paper: hedge orders are
  simulated at the hedge's last price plus the paper costs. Both paper traders stream the hedge's prices (IBKR: the
  fund on SMART and the overnight venue, or the front-month future; Webull: the fund).
* Live: its own switch (`POST /api/plus/hedge/algo/live`), typed confirmation HEDGE LIVE, refused unless `./jb live`
  runs for that algo, and tied to that run's `logs/LIVE_RUNNING` (pid and time): a new `./jb live` starts with live
  hedging off. Live hedge orders go through each broker's own `live_broker.place_order` (IBKR: tagged
  `orderRef jb-hedge`; a futures hedge is checked at its full size and needs no OVERNIGHT route); a `jb-hedge` order
  still open at IBKR stops `./jb live` from starting; at start-up the hedge record is compared with the broker.
* Safety, all tested: Stop button and daily loss limit (KILL) close the hedge and open nothing; while SELL ALL runs
  (KILL by sellall) no hedge order at all, and the hedge is booked closed once the broker shows it gone; SELL ALL
  closes fund and futures hedges (its futures limits now sit on the 0.25 price step: `gateway/killswitch.py`);
  buying power check; max order value (the hedge is cut down to fit, or not sent when one contract is too big); speed
  limits and the duplicate check count hedge orders (a hedge never counts as a duplicate of the position's own order);
  a rejected hedge: no new hedge for 15 minutes, counts toward 3 rejections in a row (live trading stops); an
  unconfirmed hedge order pauses live trading; halted still lets a hedge close; the hedge is never left open after the
  position closes (also when Auto-Hedge is turned off) and never sells more than the broker holds; futures hedges are
  refused on the Webull algo; no futures orders in CME's 5-6 pm pause.
* The hedge's result (realized since 8 pm ET + open) counts toward the algo's daily loss limit
  (`gateway/trading.py _algo_today`).
* Files: `algo/autohedge.py`, `algo/core_trader.py`, both `algo/paper_trader.py`, `ibkr-algo/algo/live_broker.py`,
  `gateway/hedge.py`, `gateway/trading.py`, `gateway/killswitch.py`, `web/hedge-algo.js`, `web/legend.js`.
* Tests: `tests/algo-stress-test.py` (Auto-Hedge section, both brokers) and `tests/gateway-fake-broker-test.py`
  (SELL ALL closes hedges on IBKR incl. MNQ and on Webull; settings and the live switch; loss limit with the hedge).

## Part 6: Webull leftover hedge at start-up (follow-up 1)

Webull now finds a hedge order left by an earlier run, as IBKR does with its `jb-hedge` tag.

* Every live hedge order (both algos) is written down in `logs/hedge_state_live.json` (`orders`) **before** it is sent,
  with a client order id that starts with `jbhedge` (Webull sends that id; 32 characters as before).
* `./jb live webull ...` start-up (`webull-algo/algo/paper_trader.py`, `_hedge_leftovers`): one read of every open
  Webull order. It refuses to start while an Auto-Hedge order is still open: one whose id starts with `jbhedge`, one of
  the recorded ids, or any order for a hedge fund (the open hedge, the chosen one while Auto-Hedge is on, those of
  unanswered hedge orders). Recorded orders without a final answer are also looked up one by one (this works even
  when the open-order list can't be read).
* A recorded hedge order that filled while the algo was off is adopted (booked as the algo's hedge for the position it
  was sent for; the next tick closes it if that position is gone). Hedge-fund shares the account holds beyond the
  record are reported on the algo tab and block a second hedge while they are there (re-checked every 30 s; the algo
  never sells them). Fewer than the record: booked closed, as before.
* IBKR start-up now also reads the chosen hedge's position while Auto-Hedge is on (same report). Paper: the saved paper
  hedge is kept after a restart, with a note on the tab.
* Files: both `algo/autohedge.py`, `webull-algo/algo/live_broker.py` (`open_orders`, `order_state`, the recorded id),
  both `algo/paper_trader.py`, `web/legend.js`.
* Tests: `tests/algo-stress-test.py` (leftover hedge section, both brokers; the Webull start-up itself against the fake
  Webull client: tagged order, untagged order for the hedge fund, unreadable list, fill adopted, shares reported).

## Part 7: Webull at the IBKR algo's level (follow-up 2)

`webull-algo/algo/paper_trader.py` compared with `ibkr-algo/algo/paper_trader.py`; Webull now does what IBKR does
wherever Webull's API allows.

* **Prices stopped:** the IBKR wait times per session (5 min regular hours, 15 min pre-market and after-hours, 30 min
  overnight when the plan streams overnight). The stream is reconnected each time; the phone gets
  "Webull algo: prices stopped" once per outage (again only after prices came back) when the algo may trade in that
  session or holds a position (urgent while it holds one). Before: a reconnect after 5 min, 4 am - 8 pm, no push.
* **Connection lost (IBKR's 1100):** Webull's streaming client is an MQTT client (paho-mqtt, checked in the real SDK
  3.0.3): its `on_disconnect` callback with a non-zero code means the link dropped (the SDK reconnects by itself every
  10 s, so before this the algo never noticed). Now: `connected` off, so the existing safe mode holds orders until
  the stream is subscribed again for a minute and the position matches Webull. Webull's notice message type 1002
  ("permission preemption": another session took over the market data) does the same until prices come back.
* **Short sales:** the real SDK has the order side `SHORT` (`webull.trade.common.order_side`), and Webull's API
  reference lists BUY / SELL / SHORT. Enabled for live: an entry of a strategy with `entry: side: sell` goes out as
  SHORT; a cover is a BUY; closing a long is a SELL. Refuses to start unless the account is a margin account (from
  Webull's account list) and unless Webull's own order preview (`order_v3.preview_order`, never placed) accepts a
  1-share short sale; a preview that can't be made is only noted. Same checks as buys (buying power, the order
  value cap, speed limits, safe mode, guards). Never overnight: Webull takes no short sales in its overnight session,
  so paper, live and Webull backtests skip short entries then (`core_trader.no_short_sessions`,
  `run_backtest(no_short_sessions=...)`). A short sale from a buy strategy is refused in `live_broker.py`.
* **Account:** `WEBULL_ACCOUNT_ID` must be one of the API key's accounts (IBKR checks `IBKR_ACCOUNT_ID` the same way).
* Algo tab: the note line under the trades shows the trader's own notes (`broker_notes`, e.g. the short-sale rule).
* Files: `webull-algo/algo/paper_trader.py`, `webull-algo/algo/live_broker.py`, both `algo/core_trader.py` and
  `algo/backtest.py`, `webull-algo/run_backtest.py`, `web/algo.html` (one line), `web/legend.js`,
  `webull-algo/README.md`, the fake Webull SDK (`on_disconnect`, `preview_order`).
* Tests: `tests/algo-stress-test.py` (Webull at the IBKR level section).

## Part 8: Go Live button (follow-up 3)

Live trading for a broker without Terminal: a background job started from the algo tab. `./jb live` keeps working.

* **Card** *Live Trading (Real Money)* on each algo tab (`web/golive-ui.js`, one script line in `web/algo.html`), under
  the top two cards: the strategy the paper algo runs (`WEBULL_ALGO_STRATEGY` / `IBKR_ALGO_STRATEGY`) and whether its
  file has `confirm_live: true` and `risk: max_order_notional_usd`; the go-live checklist (`bin/golive.py`, the same
  checks `./jb live` prints, with the same "you can still go live, but fix the !! lines first" note); a box to type
  I_UNDERSTAND_THE_RISK; **Go Live** (asks once more); **Stop Live**; the option *Start Live Trading Again After A
  Mac Restart* (off by default); how the last live run ended and the live position it left (from the live record);
  the live job's latest log lines.
* **Refused before anything starts** (`gateway/livectl.py`, `POST /api/algo/<broker>/live`): no JB_PASSWORD (the
  gateway already refuses algo changes without one), the words not typed exactly, a live run already going (Go Live or
  `./jb live`), the strategy file without `confirm_live: true` or the order cap, or not a valid strategy.
* **Start** (`./jb live-service start <broker> <strategy> <0|1>`): writes `logs/LIVE_SERVICE` (the request, with the
  typed confirmation), stops the paper algo, and bootstraps launchd job `com.josephsboard.<broker>-live` from
  `logs/launchd/` (KeepAlive off; not in `~/Library/LaunchAgents`, so no start at login unless the option is on, which
  also writes a copy there). The job (`bin/run-service.sh <broker>-live`) sets `logs/LIVE_RUNNING` (+ `.by`), exports
  `<BROKER>_LIVE_TRADING_CONFIRM` for itself only, and runs `python -m algo.paper_trader --mode live` -- whose own
  start-up checks (an order or Auto-Hedge order still open, the position, the account...) refuse exactly as with
  `./jb live` (exit code 3; the card shows the reason from the log).
* **Stop Live** (`./jb live-service stop <broker>`): `logs/LIVE_STOP`, then launchd's SIGTERM; the trader now treats
  SIGTERM like Ctrl+C (orders still working cancelled, nothing sent, state saved, the position stays). The paper algo
  starts again. A Terminal run is stopped with Ctrl+C there (the card says so).
* **Stops on its own**: `logs/LIVE_EXITED` -> the guardian's loud push (texts now name Go Live); `LIVE_RUNNING` gone
  while the job is dead (power cut) -> the existing "LIVE trader is NOT running" push. A start-up refusal right after
  Go Live is shown on the card instead of pushed; one at login (option on) is pushed. The paper algo starts again.
* **LIVE shown**: red banner on the algo tab while it runs, red banner on the Phone page, LIVE badge on the algo tab
  buttons (`/api/algo/live`; one script line each in `web/phone.html` and `web/index.html`). `./jb status` lists it.
* Texts that said live only runs in Terminal now say Go Live or `./jb live` (Use This Strategy, live hedging).
* Files: `gateway/livectl.py` (new), `gateway/gateway.py` (route), `gateway/guardian.py`, `gateway/algoctl.py`,
  `gateway/hedge.py`, `jb` (`live-service`, status, uninstall), `bin/run-service.sh` (`<broker>-live`), both

## Part 9: Webull overnight ready (follow-up 4)

Everything works by itself the moment Webull starts sending overnight prices (the overnight data plan); until then it
works as before. Futures stay IBKR only.

* **Re-check (no restart needed):** the Webull board bridge, the scanner feed and the Webull algo switched overnight
  off after Webull refused it, until the next restart. Now each asks again every 3 hours and at 7:45 pm New York
  before a trading day (`lib/jb_webull.py overnight_recheck_due`): one subscription of one stock that asks for the
  overnight session. Taken: overnight is on (the board's "no overnight prices" notice goes and its tiles show
  overnight prices, the stream reconnects so every symbol gets them, the algo trades overnight again when its Trading
  options allow). So after buying the plan **no `./jb restart` is needed**: it starts within 3 hours, or at 7:45 pm ET
  at the latest that evening (a `./jb restart` makes it immediate).
* **On or off right now:** each part writes what it found to `data/webull_overnight.json` (the newest answer wins);
  `GET /api/algo/webull` returns it (`overnight_data`). The Webull algo tab's note line says *Overnight Prices
  (Webull): On / Off*; the ? Legend on the Webull Algo and Webull tabs shows it live.
* **Algo overnight:** trades the overnight session when its Trading options allow and the plan has it. The
  "prices stopped" push waits 30 min overnight (Part 7). Short sales never overnight (Part 7).
* **Backup stop overnight:** overnight the algo asks for the stop in Webull's NIGHT session (a DAY order, as Webull
  takes overnight). If Webull refuses, a day stop (all sessions, GTC) is placed instead and no night one is asked for
  for a week (`logs/webull_caps.json`). The core switches stops at 8 pm and 3:50 am via `stop_api['fits']`: the old
  one is cancelled first and the new one placed only after the broker confirms -- never two at once.
* **Auto-Hedge overnight (inverse funds):** hedge orders go to the NIGHT session when overnight is allowed; if the
  broker refuses one overnight (e.g. a fund not on Webull's 24-hour list), no more hedge orders until 4 am (one
  rejection instead of three, which would stop live trading). Shared code, so IBKR gets it too.
* **Overnight gap strategy for Webull:** `webull-algo/strategies/overnight_gap.yaml`, the same rules as IBKR's;
  backtests on Webull data (`--webull`; the history asks for pre-market, after-hours and overnight, and keeps
  pre-market and after-hours when the plan refuses overnight -- before, a refusal fell back to regular hours only) or
  on the Webull board's record (`--recorded`).
* **Gateway follows the plan:** the Health tab expects Webull prices overnight only once the plan has them; Hedge
  Ideas may be sent to the Webull algo for the overnight session then; the order ticket's and Flip's "your Webull plan
  has no overnight prices" warnings only while it has none; the hedge calculator's hours line says so.
